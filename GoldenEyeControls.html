GoldenEye 007

Controls:

  • Enter - Start
  • Tab - Shoot
  • Q or E - Aim
  • T - Move forward
  • G - Move Backward
  • F - Turn Left
  • H - Turn Right
  • Left Arrow OR J - Stride Left
  • Right Arrow or L - Stride Right
  • Up Arrow or I- Look down
  • Down Arrow OR k - Look up
  • X - Use Fist
  • 1 - Quick Save State
